## DynaIsland 1.0.0

The first release: an Apple-style Dynamic Island at the top of your Windows screen.

- **Now playing:** album art, animated bars, and a hover-to-expand player with seek and controls. Swipe to skip tracks. Works with Spotify, browsers, Media Player and more.
- **Timer, focus sessions and stopwatch**, with an iPhone-style side bubble while music plays.
- **Notifications** mirrored with the app's icon, plus **calendar** heads-ups from classic Outlook.
- **Volume and mute** (speakers and microphone), **brightness**, **battery**, **Caps Lock** and **keyboard layout**.
- **Bluetooth** devices (with battery), **network and VPN**, **mic and camera in use** dots, and **Do Not Disturb**.
- **Screenshots** with a thumbnail, **downloads**, and **clipboard** copies.
- Themes, sizes, screen choice, a tray icon, start with Windows, and a **low-memory mode** (about 29 MB).

Everything runs locally: no network access, no telemetry, no accounts.
